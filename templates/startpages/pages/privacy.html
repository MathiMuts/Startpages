Privacy

Last updated {% now "F Y" %}.

Startpages is a personal homepage builder. This page explains what it stores, why, and what you can do about it. In short: your pages are yours, they're visible only to you, and nothing you save is sold or used to advertise to you.

What we store

  • Your account. A username and email address so you can sign in. If you sign in with Google, we store the account identifier and email Google returns.
  • Your pages. The startpages, sections, links, colours and theme you create. This is the content of the app.
  • Your avatar. A profile image, only if you upload one.

Cookies

We use a small number of first-party cookies, and no third-party or advertising cookies:

  • A session cookie to keep you signed in, and a CSRF cookie to protect form submissions.
  • A theme cookie that remembers your chosen colours so the page doesn't flash on load.

How your data is used

Your content is used only to render your startpages back to you. We don't run ad tracking or analytics on the links you save. The site operator may receive an internal notification when a new account is registered, used only to keep an eye on the service — never shared.

Your choices

  • Export. Every page can be downloaded as a JSON file from your profile at any time.
  • Delete. You can delete individual pages yourself. To remove your account and all associated data, contact the site operator.
  • Disconnect. Linked Google accounts can be disconnected from your profile.

Contact

Questions about your data, or want your account removed? Reach out to the site operator using the contact address listed for this installation.
